Source transformation
  apply_ce_sop_candidate.py starts from the pinned pre-optimization reference,
  removes only assignments to held/pending/release/delivered/blocked bitmaps,
  and inserts their independent per-bit processes. UART, FIFO data/pointers/count,
  mapping lookup/commit, parser, scan scheduling, controls and panic detection
  remain the original text. The reference remains byte-for-byte unchanged.

Why this differs from the unsuccessful full-vector next-state attempt
  held/pending/delivered share one scalar D value per bitmap and use local CE.
  Thus normal point writes do not feed each old bitmap bit through a D mux.
  release/blocked are scalar-per-bit SOPs with priorities gated into global
  controls. They do not cascade 128-bit ternary masks for each priority level.
  Savings are a synthesis hypothesis, not a certified LUT count; parent owns
  the full-toolchain comparison and routing/fit decision.

Equivalence reasoning (all RHS state is OLD, no additional pipeline)
  held: global clear = panic | entering_suppress | CC123. Otherwise only accepted
  On or any Off updates the addressed bit; D is accepted_on after global clear.
  pending: same point-write enable, with sustain_up added to global clear. D is
  deferred_off after global clear. An accepted On clears an existing pending Off.
  delivered: panic clears every bit; otherwise pop changes only event_raw_note
  to event_on. The FIFO fields and raw metadata are unchanged.
  release: panic gates every SOP term off. Suppress entry selects OLD delivered
  corrected by same-edge pop On/Off. Otherwise CC123 ORs OLD held/pending;
  sustain_up ORs OLD pending; accepted On clears its raw bit; successful scan
  clears its scan bit. CC123/sustain_up/accepted On are mutually exclusive MIDI
  message decodes, and scan_offer already excludes these and suppress entry.
  blocked: panic with suppress keeps OLD blocked|held, panic without suppress
  clears all. During panic, same-edge key edits are disabled explicitly. Without
  panic, suppress entry imports OLD held, Off clears its raw bit, and a
  suppressed On sets its raw bit. Thus pending-only notes do not become blocked
  when panic occurs, and an old held key is not accidentally lost.
